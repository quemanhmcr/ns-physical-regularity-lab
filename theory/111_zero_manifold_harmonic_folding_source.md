# Harmonic folding of a degenerate zero manifold

Spectral ancestry does not control geometry. A material zero line can be folded without changing `B` eigenvalues.

For a material curve, with tangent `T=X_s` and second derivative `K=X_ss`,

`D_t T=A T`,

`D_t K=A K+(grad A)[T,T]`.

Thus an initially straight line acquires curvature only from the velocity Hessian. Affine strain cannot fold it.

But an irrotational non-affine actor can. The harmonic potentials

`phi_m=-Re[(x+i z)^(m+1)]/(m+1)`

have zero vorticity and divergence-free velocity. On the z-axis their transverse velocity is a monomial `z^m`; the straight line therefore receives curvature source

`|D_t K|=m(m-1)|z|^(m-2)`.

So fixed-time multiplicity can in principle be created without any local vorticity ancestry mutation. The price cannot be sought in null enstrophy. It lives in the Hodge-harmonic velocity field that performs the folding.

A nontrivial global finite-energy harmonic field cannot occupy all of space. In a real Navier-Stokes field this local harmonic actor must be sourced by vorticity outside the ball. The next physical gate is its finite-energy Hodge occupancy and the shrinking source radius needed to sustain increasingly fine folding.
