# Winding gain versus transverse viscous persistence

The maintenance transaction/lobe separation leaves a dangerous escape: perhaps a material lineage with circulation `Gamma << nu` can wind `N >>1` times and still supply the order-one transaction Reynolds required by a shrinking screened core.

The exact spherical-shell slow spiral already gives the right adversarial test.

At the characteristic latitude `theta=pi/4`,

`omega_r=A/(2 sqrt(2) r^2)`,

`omega_phi=B/(2r)`.

Its positive throughgoing circulation is

`Gamma=pi A/2`,

while an `N`-turn traverse obeys

`B/A=pi N/DeltaR`.

The characteristic Hodge transaction circulation is

`Gamma_Q=2Br/5`.

Thus winding gain is

`Gamma_Q/Gamma=(4/5) N r/DeltaR`.

## The transverse flux-area clock

At the same physical point define the characteristic flux-area scale

`A_Gamma = Gamma/|omega|`.

This is the area dimension carried by circulation flux divided by local vorticity magnitude. Its natural viscous clock is

`tau_nu=A_Gamma/nu`.

The transaction strain is `q=Gamma_Q/r^2`, so

`tau_trans=r^2/Gamma_Q`.

Direct substitution gives the exact shell identity

`tau_nu/tau_trans = (4/5)(Gamma/nu) [1+DeltaR^2/(2 pi^2 N^2 r^2)]^-1/2`.

Therefore

`tau_nu/tau_trans -> (4/5) Gamma/nu`

as `N->infinity`.

The winding leverage cancels out of the persistence clock. The same geometry that raises transaction per unit circulation raises local vorticity and shrinks the corresponding transverse flux-area scale.

## Fixed transaction attack

Hold `Gamma_Q/nu=R_Q>0` fixed and let the lineage Reynolds `R_Gamma=Gamma/nu` tend to zero.

Then

`N ~ R_Q/R_Gamma -> infinity`,

but

`tau_nu/tau_trans ~ (4/5) R_Gamma ->0`.

So the lineage can create the **instantaneous** transaction by arbitrarily large winding, but in this exact calibration it cannot remain viscously coherent for even one transaction clock as its own circulation Reynolds vanishes.

This is precisely the distinction that the static transaction geometry could not see.

## Scope

The identity is exact for the validated spherical-shell throughgoing geometry. `A_Gamma=Gamma/|omega|` is a characteristic transverse flux-area scale at the representative point, not a universal lower bound for every distorted material tube.

Hence the result does not yet close the general ancestry problem. It does, however, kill the simplest reuse mechanism:

`vanishing lineage circulation + infinite winding + no renewal`.

The remaining possibilities are now dynamical:

1. relay the transaction among fresh material lineages before each one diffuses;
2. use viscosity itself to renew ancestry fast enough;
3. exploit nonuniform tube geometry that changes the transverse persistence law;
4. use a genuinely different nonlinear maintenance actor.

The next microscope should therefore count **ancestry relay/renewal events**, not circulation stock and not static winding number.
