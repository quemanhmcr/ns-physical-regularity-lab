# Obstruction ledger 03: the coherence-length escape route

The first two exact canonical stress-tests reveal the same local critical currency:

- material-lineage persistence under strain: a `Gamma^2` toll **per unit axial length** per strain time;
- material-lineage switching by viscous diffusion: a positive `Gamma^2` toll **per unit axial length** for a fixed fractional circulation transfer, even after localizing the charge to an annulus around the crossing material boundary.

This is physically meaningful but is **not yet enough for 3D regularity**.

## Why the global energy budget still has an escape route

A three-dimensional coherent segment of axial length `L` converts the per-length toll into an actual energy expenditure of the form

`C_3D ~ c Gamma^2 L`.

If a hypothetical singular cascade is allowed to move to coherent segments whose axial length shrinks with the transverse scale,

`L_j ~ ell_j`,

then even with a scale-critical circulation floor `Gamma_j >= c nu`, the event costs can behave like

`C_j ~ c nu^2 ell_j`.

For a dyadic cascade `ell_j ~ 2^{-j}`, the series `sum_j C_j` is finite.  This is precisely the supercritical loophole that an energy-budget-only proof cannot close.

Therefore the canonical `Gamma^2` result should be interpreted as a **local transaction law**, not yet as the non-summable global toll promised by the regularity program.

## The next physical question

A singularly viable three-dimensional core must not only carry circulation; it must also acquire the strain that amplifies it.  Strain is generated nonlocally by the surrounding vorticity geometry.  The next candidate obstruction is therefore:

> Can a core keep `Gamma/nu = O(1)` or larger while both its transverse scale and the spatial extent of the vorticity/strain structure needed to amplify it collapse proportionally, without paying an additional nonlocal geometric or pressure-mediated cost?

This shifts the program from *circulation accounting alone* to *circulation + strain-support geometry*.

The Perelman-style interpretation is important: we do not hide this escape route behind a stronger norm.  We promote it to the next object of study.  The desired next mechanism must explain why the singularity cannot repeatedly buy strong local stretching from an arbitrarily tiny donor geometry at arbitrarily high efficiency.
