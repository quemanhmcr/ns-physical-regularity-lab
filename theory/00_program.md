# Physical program v0

We treat a hypothetical finite-time singularity as a physical production process rather than as the statement that a norm diverges.

Core chain:

1. vorticity amplification requires sustained stretching;
2. incompressibility converts stretching into transverse compression somewhere in material geometry;
3. smaller transverse scales shorten the viscous diffusion time;
4. a singularly viable cascade must retain enough circulation/coherence to keep nonlinear transfer competitive with viscosity;
5. therefore each logarithmic scale transition should either pay an irreversible toll or fall below a survival threshold.

Immediate escape routes that must be attacked rather than assumed away:

- circulation can decay with scale;
- singular activity can switch material lineage;
- strain can be supplied nonlocally from larger scales;
- the geometry may be sheet-like, tube-like, or more general;
- the same physical resource must not be double-counted across scales.

The repository exists to falsify and sharpen this program before any claim of proof.
