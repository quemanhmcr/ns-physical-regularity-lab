# Localized productive spectral gap: source-scale renewal versus frozen ancestry

## THINK — repair the diffusion clock after discovering the smooth zero mode

The old observation-radius clock `r^2/nu` was too crude because the sharp productive carrier has a smooth radial viscosity-null mode

`Q=r^2 C`.

A small observation radius inside such a profile does not imply a feature varying on that radius.  The physically meaningful diffusion scale must therefore be the radius at which the productive transaction profile is actually **localized** or tapered.

Let `L` be such a source radius and consider a regular productive radial profile satisfying

`Q(L)=0`.

Write

`Q=r^2 K(r)`.

Then `K(L)=0` and the radial operator factorization gives an exact Dirichlet form.

## Exact localized defect form

Integration by parts gives

`integral_0^L r^2 Q:(-L_2 Q) dr`

`= integral_0^L r^6 |K'(r)|^2 dr`.

The cancellation of all zero-mode terms is structural.  The quantity on the right measures only radial departure from the circulation-preserving smooth profile.

## Universal source-scale spectral gap

Since `K(L)=0`,

`K(r)=-integral_r^L K'(s) ds`.

Weighted Cauchy gives

`|K(r)|^2 <= [integral_r^L s^6 |K'|^2 ds] [integral_r^L s^-6 ds]`.

Integrating this estimate with weight `r^6` yields

`integral_0^L r^6 |K|^2 dr`

`<= (L^2/14) integral_0^L r^6 |K'|^2 dr`.

Therefore

`integral r^2 Q:(-L_2 Q) dr`

`>= (14/L^2) integral r^2 |Q|^2 dr`.

The constant `14` is not claimed sharp; it is a simple exact coercive constant generated by the physical localization boundary and degree-two geometry.  The true first Dirichlet eigenvalue is larger, but no spectral root is needed for the mechanism.

## Pure viscous consequence

If only viscosity acts on this localized productive sector,

`partial_t Q = nu L_2 Q`,

then

`d/dt integral r^2 |Q|^2 dr`

`<= -(28nu/L^2) integral r^2 |Q|^2 dr`.

Thus the squared productive norm decays at least as

`exp[-28nu t/L^2]`.

This is not a claim that the full Navier-Stokes `Q` obeys a homogeneous heat equation.  Nonlinear deformation can continually source the sector.  The estimate instead gives the rate that such regeneration must outrun.

## Source-scale transaction Reynolds gate

Let `s` be the productive strain rate that the localized source must supply, with production time

`tau_prod=1/s`.

The natural source-scale number is

`Re_source = s L^2/nu`.

Over one production time the pure-diffusion squared-norm survival ceiling is

`exp[-28/Re_source]`.

Therefore a localized productive event has two physical branches:

1. **renewal-dominated / low source Re**: `Re_source` is small, so viscous ancestry curvature erodes the productive sector within a production time and nonlinear/material regeneration must continually rebuild it;
2. **approximately frozen / high source Re**: the event acts faster than the universal localized diffusion clock, so circulation ancestry is approximately preserved over that production interval and material deformation/occupancy becomes the relevant branch.

This is the repaired transaction Reynolds microscope.  It uses the source localization scale selected by the productive profile itself and automatically excludes the nonlocalized `r^2` harmonic escape.
