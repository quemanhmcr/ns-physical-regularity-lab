# Vorticity-to-strain transaction microscope

## 1. Physics first: ask what vorticity the Hodge microscope actually sees

At a center `x0`, the Hodge microscope already separates

`S(x0) = S_h(r) + S_v(r)`

using the actual normal velocity on the physical sphere `partial B_r(x0)`.  The next question is not how large the vorticity norm is inside the ball.  It is:

> Which signed angular part of the vorticity is actually transacted into `S_v(r)` after all Biot-Savart cancellation has occurred?

Write `y-x0 = rho n`, `|n|=1`, and let `omega(rho,n)` be the actual vorticity.  Define the shell transaction tensor

`Q(rho) = [3/(8 pi)] integral_{S^2} [ n tensor (n cross omega) + (n cross omega) tensor n ] dOmega`.

`Q` is symmetric and trace free.  It keeps the full angular sign and tensor structure of the Biot-Savart strain kernel.  It is not an absolute-value bound.  Vorticity that is large but cancels vectorially or angularly is invisible to `Q`.

The natural circulation-dimensional version is

`Gamma_Q(rho) = rho^2 Q(rho)`.

This is a quadrupolar circulation transaction, not a claim that one Kelvin loop alone measures the whole tensor.

## 2. Exact Hodge-screened Biot-Savart identity

Let `t=log r`.  The Hodge harmonic strain profile obeys the exact radial equation

`(partial_t^2 + 5 partial_t) S_h(r) = -5 Q(r)`.

Equivalently, using smoothness `S_h(r)->S(x0)` and `partial_t S_h(r)->0` as `r->0`, the vortical Hodge remainder is

`S_v(r) = integral_0^r [1-(rho/r)^5] Q(rho) d rho/rho`.

The factor `1-(rho/r)^5` is not an analyst's cutoff.  It is the Green weight generated by the degree-two Hodge boundary problem itself.  Vorticity arbitrarily close to the observation boundary is screened because that part can be represented at the center by the harmonic boundary response; deep interior vorticity approaches the ordinary Biot-Savart weight.

A direct derivation starts from

`I(r)=integral_{S^2} u_r(r,n)(n tensor n-I/3)dOmega`,

`S_h=(15/(8pi r)) I`.

Using incompressibility to eliminate the degree-two tangential-velocity moment and the identity

`n cross omega = grad u_r - u_t/r - partial_r u_t`,

one obtains the radial ODE above.  No global support assumption and no Fourier partition is used.

This immediately closes one escape route: **nonlocal vorticity can influence the total center strain, but at a fixed Hodge radius its contribution is in `S_h`; the part called `S_v(r)` is exactly a screened transaction of the vorticity inside that physical ball.**

## 3. Productive self-stretching is a scalar signed transaction

For a chosen center direction `e`, define the singularity-relevant shell transaction

`q_e(rho) = e . Q(rho) e`.

Because `Q` is trace free this is also `(e tensor e-I/3):Q`.  The exact accumulated vortical stretching component is

`e.S_v(r)e = integral_0^r [1-(rho/r)^5] q_e(rho) d rho/rho`.

Writing `omega=varpi xi`,

`q_e(rho) = [3/(4pi)] integral_{S^2} varpi (e.n) e.(n cross xi) dOmega`.

This formula exposes the productive geometry before any norm estimate is introduced.  If the surrounding vorticity direction is everywhere exactly `xi=e`, then

`q_e(rho)=0`

regardless of how large `varpi` is.  A perfectly coherent same-direction vorticity cloud can carry circulation and rotation, but it cannot self-transact strain onto that same direction through this shell kernel.

The scalar signed decomposition also makes cancellation observable.  Positive and negative `q_e` on different angular sectors or radii subtract before they reach `S_v`.  A "gross vorticity" count therefore overstates productive content; the net transaction is what the PDE uses.

## 4. Canonical attack: coherent rotation plus a productive strain carrier

Take a ball of radius `L`, `e=e_z`, and

`S = s diag(-1,-1,2)`.

Use the exact tangent strain carrier

`u_c(x) = (1-5|x|^2/(3L^2)) Sx + [2/(3L^2)] (x.S.x)x`

and superpose rigid rotation

`u_R(x) = (W/2) e cross x`.

The rotation changes the vorticity coherence but contributes no strain.  The carrier has

`omega_c = -(14/(3L^2)) x cross Sx = (14 s/L^2) z (e cross x)`.

On a sphere of radius `r`, `x=r n`, its exact transaction is

`Q_c(r) = (14/5)(r/L)^2 S`,

so

`q_e(r) = (28/5) s (r/L)^2`.

The background `W e` contributes **exactly zero** to `Q` and `q_e`.

The Hodge profile remains

`S_h(r)=[1-(r/L)^2]S`,

`S_v(r)=(r/L)^2 S`.

Thus the half-strain Hodge radius is `r=L/sqrt(2)`, and the productive shell transaction there is fixed while `W` can be varied independently.

This family kills the naive hypothesis

> fixed productive self-stretching implies a universal positive lower bound on `varpi |grad xi|^2` independent of circulation.

Indeed, as `W->infinity`, the total vorticity direction becomes arbitrarily coherent with `e` while `q_e` is unchanged, and the directional tax tends to zero.  The escape is real.  But it is not free: it spends coherent background circulation and kinetic energy.

## 5. Exact angular geometry-tax bracket

On the sphere, write `mu=e.n` and

`A = 14 s (r/L)^2 = (5/2) q_e`.

Then

`omega = W e + A mu sqrt(1-mu^2) e_phi`,

`M(mu)=|omega|=[W^2+A^2 mu^2(1-mu^2)]^(1/2)`.

For `xi=omega/M`, the angular part of the exact directional tax has sphere average

`D_ang(r) = (1/(4pi)) integral |omega| |grad_{S^2} xi|^2 dOmega`.

Its integrand is exactly

`W^2 A^2 (2mu^2-1)^2 / M^3 + A^2 mu^2/M`.

Let `epsilon=A/W`.  Since `mu^2(1-mu^2)<=1/4`,

`(A^2/W) c_-(epsilon) <= D_ang <= (4/5)(A^2/W)`,

where

`c_-(epsilon) = [7/15](1+epsilon^2/4)^(-3/2) + [1/3](1+epsilon^2/4)^(-1/2)`.

As `epsilon->0`, `c_->4/5`.  Therefore in the coherent branch

`(nu/r^2) D_ang ~ 5 nu q_e^2/(W r^2)`.

The full physical tax `nu |omega| |grad xi|^2` is at least this angular contribution; radial variation can only add cost.

This is the first repaired gain-cost law supplied by the transaction microscope:

- gain is linear in the signed productive transaction `q_e`,
- coherence suppression of geometry tax uses a background vorticity `W`,
- the tax falls only like `1/W`,
- the required coherent resource rises with `W`.

For the rigid component inside `B_r`,

`Gamma_R = pi W r^2`,

`E_R(B_r)=pi W^2 r^5/15 = Gamma_R^2 r/(15pi)`.

For the axisymmetric carrier, `u_R` is azimuthal while `u_c` is poloidal, so they are pointwise orthogonal and this rotational energy cannot be hidden by a negative cross term.

Consequently, for any declared coherence level `epsilon<=epsilon_0`,

`[(nu/r^2)D_ang] sqrt(E_R)`

has the lower bound

`(25/4)c_-(epsilon_0) nu q_e^2 sqrt(pi r/15)`.

In the strong-coherence limit the coefficient tends to `5 sqrt(pi/15)`.

This law is exact for this canonical family.  It is not yet a theorem for arbitrary Navier-Stokes geometry.

## 6. Escape-route autopsy

### Alignment

Perfect same-direction alignment kills `q_e`; near alignment can keep `q_e` fixed only by increasing the coherent background amplitude.  The directional tax can therefore be bought down, but the price is circulation/energy.  **Naive universal tax: killed.  Resource-coupled tax in the canonical family: survives.**

### Vector/tensor cancellation

`Q` and `q_e` are signed.  Opposite productive sectors or bands can cancel exactly.  In the coherent canonical family the angular tax is quadratic in transaction amplitude to leading order, so adding opposite transactions increases cost for a fixed net gain.  This is evidence against cancellation as a free route in that family, not a general convexity theorem for all flows.

### Beltrami-type geometry

The earlier exact helical Beltrami calibration has nonzero direction tax but zero stretching.  The present microscope explains why it is not an escape: directional complexity by itself is not productive; only the signed kernel projection `q_e` matters.

### Nonlocal interaction

At a fixed Hodge radius, nonlocal influence is harmonic and belongs to `S_h`.  Once the transition scale forces `S_v=O(s)`, that portion must be financed by interior `Q`.  **Direct nonlocal escape from the vortical branch: killed by representation, not by a decay estimate.**

### Shrinking productive support

If a fixed net transaction is concentrated into a logarithmic radial width `delta`, then its shell amplitude scales like `q_e~1/delta`.  In the coherent branch the volume-integrated angular tax scales like `delta q_e^2`, hence like `1/delta`; radial tapering adds further nonnegative direction tax.  Shrinking support is therefore penalized in this family unless the coherent resource `W` is increased at the same time, which moves the burden into circulation/energy.

### Material-lineage switching

The microscope is Eulerian at an instant.  It identifies who pays for the strain transaction at that instant, but it does not yet prove that the same material lineage must keep paying.  A cascade may attempt to replace taxed donors by fresh ones.  **This escape remains open.**  The next frontier must add time/history to the transaction observable without destroying its Hodge-defined physical scale.

## 7. Promotion status

Promote the following statements:

1. `Q(r)` is the exact signed angular/quadrupolar vorticity observable naturally paired with Hodge strain.
2. `S_v(r)` is the Hodge-screened radial accumulation of `Q`, with weight `1-(rho/r)^5`.
3. The singularity-relevant scalar `q_e=e.Qe` vanishes for perfectly same-direction vorticity and therefore measures genuinely productive directional departure rather than vorticity amount alone.
4. In the coherent-rotation + productive-carrier family, reducing the exact directional geometry tax at fixed productive transaction requires unbounded coherent circulation/energy; the explicit gain-cost bracket above is rigorous for that family.

Do **not** promote a universal regularity mechanism yet.  The major obstruction is temporal reuse: one must show that an infinite sequence of singularly productive Hodge-scale transactions cannot be financed by material-lineage switching or by a more exotic non-canonical geometry that evades the canonical circulation/tax tradeoff.
