# D2-fixed Hodge response: the hidden meaning of the old rank six

The stationary capacity strain has three distinct eigenaxes and therefore a proper `D2` stabilizer generated by pi rotations about those axes.

For scalar spherical harmonics of degree `l`, the fixed-space dimension is

`dim Fix_D2(H_l) = [(2l+1)+3(-1)^l]/4`.

Equivalently,

- odd `l`: `(l-1)/2`;
- even `l`: `(l+2)/2`.

This follows directly in an eigenframe: a monomial invariant under all three pi rotations has all exponents even for even `l` and all exponents odd for odd `l`; removing `r^2` times the lower polynomial gives the harmonic count.

Poloidal and toroidal Hodge sectors carry the same angular irreducible representation.  Hence the `D2`-fixed transaction-null dimension at even homogeneous degree `d=2m` is

`m^2+2m-2`.

The first values are

`d=4: 6`, `d=6: 13`, `d=8: 22`, `d=10: 33`, `d=12: 46`.

The old six-dimensional degree-four Krylov space is therefore not an accidental closure of iteration: it equals the **entire symmetry-allowed degree-four null Hodge space**.

At angular `T4`, the complete feedback space has dimension nine but its `D2`-fixed part has dimension three.  Module 143 constructs this fixed subspace from the physical vector fields by group averaging and re-certifies the coupled degree-four/six fixed point directly in the resulting three eigenframe feedback channels.

This is a representation simplification supplied by the physical strain stabilizer, not by discarding admissible symmetric dynamics.
