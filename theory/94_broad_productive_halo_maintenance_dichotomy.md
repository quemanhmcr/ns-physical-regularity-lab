# Broad productive halo maintenance dichotomy

The Oseen attack leaves a precise escape: a tiny active loop can sit deep inside a much broader circulation reservoir. Then its own local material circulation may change very slowly even though the tiny loop scale is small.

But a reservoir only matters for maintenance if it actually supplies the required **productive strain**.

Give the escape its cheapest smooth productive geometry: the exact tangent `l=2` Hodge carrier. For a carrier of radius `L`,

`S_v(r)=(r/L)^2 S_outer`.

Let the maintained core have radius `epsilon`, and write

`L=lambda epsilon`.

To supply a target maintenance strain `S_core`, the broad carrier must have

`S_outer=lambda^2 S_core`.

For the stationary productive ray,

`S_core=sigma_core S_unit`, `|S_unit|^2=21/2`.

The actual affine maintenance gate gives

`sigma_core=c nu/epsilon^2`,

with `c` an order-one angular constant.

Therefore the outer productive carrier has transaction Reynolds

`R_halo=sigma_outer L^2/nu=c lambda^4`.

So broadness itself is expensive in **source Reynolds**, even though it may protect local circulation ancestry.

Finite energy therefore does **not** eliminate the broad productive reservoir. It turns the escape into a high-Re source as the core shrinks.

## Physical dichotomy

For the minimum smooth productive carrier:

1. `lambda=O(1)`: the reservoir is core-like; exact Oseen/Burgers calibrations show order-one material-circulation renewal on local viscous clocks.
2. `lambda->infinity`: local circulation can be protected by a broad reservoir, but its own productive source Reynolds diverges as `lambda^4` and it re-enters the high-Re ancestry/cascade branch.

This is the first place where the Oseen broad-reservoir escape and the Hodge high-Re source branch become two ends of one continuous physical geometry.

## Scope

The theorem is a sharp calibration for the tangent productive carrier, not for an arbitrary vorticity reservoir. A reservoir may carry much transaction-null ancestry outside the productive sector. Such a halo can store circulation without directly paying the productive carrier formulas.
