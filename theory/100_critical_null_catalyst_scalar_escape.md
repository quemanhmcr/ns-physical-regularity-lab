# Critical null-catalyst scalar escape

Let the productive source approach a candidate singular time with

`s~tau^-1`, `epsilon~tau^alpha`,

inside the already admissible corridor

`2/5 <= alpha < 1/2`.

The validated linear null catalyst needed to maintain that source has

`A^2~s nu/epsilon^4~nu tau^(-1-4alpha)`.

Localize the catalyst with a fixed divergence-free velocity turnover shape. Since its exact kinetic occupancy scales as

`E~A^2 L^7`,

holding a finite occupancy gives

`L~tau^beta`, `beta=(1+4alpha)/7`.

Throughout the corridor `beta<1/2`.

The exact turnover-collar identity gives

`Z~A^2 L^5~tau^(-2beta)`.

Therefore

`int_0^tau nu Z dt ~ tau^(1-2beta) ->0`.

The halo energy-dissipation clock behaves as

`tau_halo~E/(nu Z)~L^2/nu~tau^(2beta)`,

so

`tau_halo/tau~tau^(2beta-1)->infinity`.

The turnover collar restores viscosity but becomes too slow, relative to the time left, to destroy the halo.

At the same finite-energy horizon the positive-cap circulation scales as

`Gamma_halo/nu~tau^(-beta/2)->infinity`.

Thus all scalar routes still permit the branch:

- bounded kinetic occupancy;
- finite, vanishing remaining viscous dissipation;
- a halo lifetime longer than the remaining singular time;
- diverging through-going circulation ancestry.

The burden has moved out of energy/dissipation and into **ancestry aggregation**: how can a smooth finite initial material circulation genealogy be reorganized so that an increasingly large through-going flux inhabits a shrinking halo?
