# Maintenance transaction versus tiny-lobe circulation

Modules 160 and 162 now describe two different physical circulation-dimensional quantities at the same shrinking core scale `epsilon`.

The first is the transaction strength of an affine actor capable of maintaining the screened core:

`R_actor = |sigma_required| epsilon^2/nu`.

The exact screened Euler ledger gives

`R_actor -> c_actor(l,orientation) > 0`.

The second is the actual Stokes circulation of one positive vorticity lobe belonging to the core itself:

`R_lobe = Gamma_lobe/nu`.

For the compact unit-screened core,

`R_lobe ~ c_lobe(l) epsilon^l/nu`.

Therefore

`R_actor/R_lobe ~ C(l,orientation) epsilon^-l`.

The core becomes increasingly incapable of financing its own maintenance: its own circulation Reynolds tends to zero while the transaction Reynolds that must be delivered to it stays order one.

## What this does not prove

`R_actor` is a Hodge/strain transaction circulation. It is not automatically the circulation of one independent material vortex lineage.

A through-going lineage can wind many times through a source and produce

`Gamma_Q/Gamma ~ N`

without increasing its circulation amount. Earlier winding modules showed exactly this and also showed that the transaction-null fraction can decrease as the winding gain increases.

So this module does **not** promote

`order-one transaction Reynolds => order-one independent material circulation stock`.

Instead it isolates the next physical question:

**Can a highly wound lineage with circulation Reynolds tending to zero repeatedly supply an order-one maintenance transaction Reynolds at shrinking scales while surviving viscosity long enough to be reused?**

That is now a lifetime/ancestry-routing problem rather than a static circulation-stock problem.
