1. **shrink the harmonic reset support scale** toward zero;

Therefore the next frontier is not to declare victory.  It is to ask whether **sustained high productivity near a fixed Gram chamber point** still requires repeated renewal of the Hodge-screened reset transaction as vorticity magnitude grows.
