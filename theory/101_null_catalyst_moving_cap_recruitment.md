# Source-relative recruitment of the null catalyst cap

For the pure linear null catalyst the positive spherical-cap circulation is

`Gamma=4pi A R^3/(3sqrt3)`.

Its self-Euler source is tangent to the sphere and its bulk Kelvin current vanishes. If the Eulerian source cap changes radius with velocity `V=Rdot n`, the exact source-relative flux law gives

`dGamma/dt = int_boundary [i_(V-u) Omega - j]`.

Direct boundary evaluation yields

`dGamma/dt = 4pi A R^2 Rdot/sqrt3 = 3 Gamma Rdot/R`.

So the moving source sees circulation change purely by material crossing. For a shrinking cap this term is negative: geometric collapse ejects active flux.

Therefore an increasing catalyst circulation in a singular branch cannot come from its self-Euler converter, and cannot come from the shrinkage itself. It requires additional external recruitment/deformation or a viscous ancestry current generated where the finite-energy field turns over.
